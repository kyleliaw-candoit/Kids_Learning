## Engagement review

The first manuscript draft was not judged strong enough to sustain confident interest from readers around ages ten to eleven. It had appealing characters and a logical premise, but low urgency, a mostly linear chase, one major reversal, and a finale that mainly confirmed information already known.

The revised manuscript retains the educational architecture and adds:

- a fictional 3:00 trophy-presentation deadline;
- explicit assurance that the learner's thinking is not timed;
- a five-stop delivery route rather than one direct trip;
- evidence that gives each Mission 3 explanation initial plausibility;
- an eleven-minute final chase after the F-5 record appears;
- useful technical contributions from Calamari;
- payoff for Cammy's interrupted duplicate-label concern;
- a Last-Car Challenge explanation for the intended parcel's label;
- an oversized-trophy/miniature-train visual reveal;
- a final evidence check by Remy before declaring the case closed.

These changes increase narrative pull through urgency, reversal, character payoff, and a more specific finale without increasing the learner's computation burden.

## Mission logic validation

### Mission 1

- A identifies DASH-3's double-chevron tread.
- B provides fresh matching tracks and a direction.
- C independently eliminates the alternate sealed passage.
- D–F are plausible but unnecessary details.
- North Concourse is the supported first investigation location.
- A+B is sufficient; C is valid checking evidence.

**Result:** Fair and solvable. The task distinguishes useful information from merely memorable information.

### Mission 2

- Five evenly positioned lines represent 0, 25, 50, 75, and 100 meters.
- From 0 to 50 there are two intervals.
- Each interval therefore represents 25 meters.
- The marker on the line between 50 and 100 represents 75 meters.
- The location board maps 75 meters to Prototype Plaza.
- Transfer scale: 10, blank, 30 produces 20 at the middle tick.

**Result:** Exact match to the approved learner observation and interval-reasoning target. The initial task does not name the arithmetic operation.

### Mission 3

- Recharge initially sounds plausible because the witness saw DASH-3 near the Charging Dock.
- It fails because current power is 78% and automatic return occurs only below 20%.
- Remote steering initially sounds plausible because the robot made three precise turns.
- It fails because the trip received zero remote commands.
- Programmed delivery agrees with DELIVERY mode and STOP 2 OF 5 — ACTIVE.

**Result:** One explanation fits the complete packet. The incorrect claims are plausible before systematic checking and fail on explicit evidence.

### Mission 4

- The route through Striped Skybridge is explicitly closed.
- Prototype Plaza → Blue Arch remains valid.
- Blue Arch → Workshop Hall → Ground Passage → Control Booth is the complete open replacement.
- The Control Booth goal remains valid.

**Result:** The learner can preserve the useful beginning and goal while revising only the blocked middle. Multiple written or drawn response forms remain valid.
