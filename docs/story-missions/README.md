# Story Missions

This folder contains the design framework, operating rules, and prototypes for **story-integrated Thinking Missions**.

The core idea is simple: a child progresses through a short, self-contained mystery or adventure by completing Thinking Missions. Each completed mission unlocks the next meaningful story development.

## Documents

- `thinking-missions-in-a-story.md` — concise overview of the concept, benefits, and core rules
- `framework.md` — detailed rationale and design philosophy for story-integrated missions
- `chapter-structure.md` — standard chapter/case rhythm and story-unlock principles
- `mission-template.md` — reusable specification for embedding one Thinking Mission into a story; includes the mandatory QA gate
- `badge-system.md` — complementary badge layer that recognizes thinking behaviors
- `case-01-upside-down-map-prototype-spec.md` — first complete story-mission prototype specification
- `case-02-requirements.md` — pre-development requirements and approval gates for Case 02
- `../Production-Quality-Gate.md` — mandatory rendered-page inspection process for every learner-facing artifact before presentation

## Working principle

Story progression is the **main motivational reward**. Badges are a supporting layer. The educational quality of the Thinking Mission always takes priority over forcing a perfect story fit.

## Release rule

A generated story-mission booklet is still a **draft** until every page has been rendered and visually inspected. It may be presented as finished only after the production quality gate is marked **QA passed**.
