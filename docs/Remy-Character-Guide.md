# Remy the Raccoon — Character Reference

This document defines the canonical Remy design for future Thinking Mission materials.

## Canonical visual reference

Use the repository image at:

`assets/remy/Remy_Character_Reference_001.jpg`

That image is the approved character sheet and should be treated as the visual source of truth when recreating or generating Remy in future chats.

## Character role

Remy is a **thinking sidekick**, not an all-knowing teacher. He models curiosity, courage, experimentation, and persistence.

He should feel like a kid's funny detective buddy who is figuring things out alongside the learner.

## Personality

Remy should be:

- curious;
- silly and slightly goofy;
- expressive;
- energetic;
- encouraging;
- brave about trying ideas;
- comfortable looking confused sometimes;
- proud of effort and persistence, not only correct answers.

Typical Remy attitudes include:

- “Hmm... what do I notice?”
- “Aha! That's interesting!”
- “Let's give it a try!”
- “Maybe another way?”
- “Mistakes are just another clue!”

## Visual style

Use a **simple, expressive character design rendered in a medium-complexity hand-drawn children's editorial style**.

Key traits:

- thick, rounded outlines with subtle hand-drawn variation;
- simple shapes and clean silhouettes;
- a limited number of purposeful internal detail lines;
- lightly textured color and restrained gentle shading;
- a few useful dimensional details, such as fur tufts, clothing folds, seams, or prop construction;
- large expressive eyes;
- exaggerated facial expressions and body poses;
- readable at small worksheet size;
- playful without looking preschool-like.

Avoid flat geometric clip art, but also avoid realistic fur rendering, dense texture, painterly shading, glossy 3D treatment, or highly detailed line work.

The character should carry more line clarity and contrast than the background. Future backgrounds should be quieter, simpler, and less textured so Remy and the story-critical objects remain the first things the learner notices.

See `Illustration-Production-Standard.md` for the complete production target and focal-hierarchy rules.

## Core appearance

Remy is a gray raccoon with:

- cream face and belly;
- dark charcoal eye mask, paws, and tail stripes;
- large expressive dark eyes;
- olive-brown detective cap;
- small green backpack/vest;
- yellow star badge;
- blue magnifying glass when investigating;
- occasional pencil/notepad props.

Accent colors may include blue, yellow, green, lavender, pink, and pale mint, matching the approved character sheet.

## Expression set

Future materials should reuse Remy in multiple emotional/thinking states rather than always showing the same pose.

Useful states include:

1. **Thinking** — chin on paw, eyes looking upward, question mark nearby.
2. **Looking for clues** — magnifying glass or notepad, alert expression.
3. **Trying an idea** — energetic jump, arms up, excited face.
4. **Keeping going** — puzzled but determined.
5. **Feeling proud** — celebrating effort with a star or badge.

## Design principle

Remy's job is to reinforce the message:

> You do not have to know the answer right away. You can notice, try, check, and try again.

The character should make productive struggle feel normal, safe, and fun.

The repository reference defines Remy's identity. The approved production treatment adds hand-drawn warmth and modest dimensionality without changing that identity.
